# Bangkok life demo: references and asset provenance

The demo explores general life-simulation mechanics: time allocation, paid work, skill development, changing needs, and short goals. Reference descriptions from the games' developer/publisher:

- Walk of Life: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1910150/Walk_of_Life/
- No Time to Relax: https://store.steampowered.com/app/829660/

ThaiNativeOnline uses its own Bangkok places, characters, written events, activities, economy, goals, and layout. Its map and characters are newly generated originals; prompts are in `assets/life-bangkok.prompt.txt` and `assets/life-characters.prompt.txt`. The convenience store uses no real brand. Do not copy third-party game artwork, sounds, code, text, character designs, logos, or distinctive interface layout into this project.
